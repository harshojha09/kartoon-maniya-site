Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

Kartoon Maniya Generator is a privately operated application for its owner. It is intended to generate original Hindi animated videos, store output files in the owner's Google Drive, and facilitate publishing to the owner's YouTube channel.

Information accessed

With the account owner's permission, the application may access Google account identifiers, Google Drive files it creates or is authorized to access, and YouTube channel/upload information as necessary for these functions. It may store OAuth credentials securely to maintain authorized access.

How information is used

Information is used only to create, store, manage and publish the owner's videos and to troubleshoot the application. It is not sold or used for advertising.

Sharing and retention

Data is shared with Google services as necessary to operate the requested features. Authorized credentials and operational records are retained only as needed to run the application, and access may be revoked in Google Account settings. Publicly published videos are subject to YouTube's policies.

Security

Credentials should be stored in private secrets, never in a public repository. No security method guarantees absolute protection.

Contact

For questions or deletion requests, contact the application owner through the contact address displayed on the Google OAuth consent screen.
